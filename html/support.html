Customer Support

We're here to help you with any questions or issues. Check out our FAQs or contact us directly.

1. How do I book a tour?
You can book a tour through our Bookings page. Select your destination, fill in details, and confirm your reservation.
2. Can I cancel my booking?
Yes, cancellations are possible up to 48 hours before your scheduled tour. Refund policies may vary based on the destination.
3. Do you offer group discounts?
Yes, we provide special discounts for groups of 5 or more. Please contact our support team for details.
4. What payment methods are accepted?
We accept all major credit cards, UPI, Debit Cards, and secure bank transfers.
